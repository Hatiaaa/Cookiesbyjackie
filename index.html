1. Scope

This policy applies to all purchases made directly through our website processed via Stripe. Orders placed through Uber Eats are subject exclusively to UberEats' refund policy and must be resolved through their platform.

2. Perishable Goods & Food Safety

All of our products are freshly baked, handcrafted perishable goods. Due to food safety regulations, we do not accept physical returns of food products once they have left our premises. This applies regardless of the reason for the return request.

However, your satisfaction is our priority. If there is a verifiable issue with your order, we will make it right through the remedies described below.

3. Eligibility for Refund or Replacement

You may be entitled to a full or partial refund, or a replacement order, in the following circumstances:

  • You received the wrong items.
  • Your order arrived significantly damaged (not due to improper storage after delivery).
  • There is a verifiable food quality issue (foreign objects, spoilage before the expected shelf life).

To submit a claim, you must:

  • Contact us at info@cookiesbyjackie.com within ★ 24 hours of receiving your order.
  • Provide your order number and at least one clear photograph of the issue.

Claims submitted after the 24-hour window, or without photographic evidence, may not be eligible for a refund.

4. Refund Process & Timelines

Once your claim is approved, we will offer one of the following remedies at our discretion:

  • Full refund to your original payment method.
  • Partial refund for partially affected orders.
  • Store credit for a future order (valid for ★ 90 days).
  • Replacement order at no additional charge.

Approved refunds are processed within ★ 5–7 business days. Depending on your bank or card issuer, the credit may take an additional 3–5 business days to appear on your statement. Refunds are always issued to the original payment method used at checkout.

5. Order Cancellations

Standard orders (cookies, pastries, cupcakes, macarons, pies):

  • Cancellations made at least ★ 24 hours before the scheduled pickup or delivery time are eligible for a full refund.
  • Same-day cancellations (less than 24 hours before fulfillment) are not eligible for a refund, as products are baked fresh to order.

Custom and specialty orders (custom cakes, large event orders, catering):

  • Cancellations made ★ 72 hours or more before the scheduled date: full refund.
  • Cancellations made between ★ 24–72 hours before: ★ 50% refund.
  • Cancellations made less than 24 hours before: no refund, as ingredients and labor have already been committed.

6. Subscription Cancellations

If Cookies by Jackie offers recurring subscription products (e.g., weekly cookie boxes, Cookie Club membership), the following terms apply:

  • Subscriptions may be cancelled at any time through your account or by emailing info@cookiesbyjackie.com.
  • To avoid being charged for the next billing cycle, cancellation must be submitted at least ★ 48 hours before your next scheduled billing date.
  • Cancellations made after the billing date has passed will take effect at the end of the current billing period. No prorated refunds are issued for the current active period.
  • All subscription cancellations will be confirmed via email.

7. Return Shipping & Logistics

Because we sell perishable food items, no physical return shipments are accepted or required. Do not ship products back to us.

For any non-food merchandise (gift packaging, branded items), returns are accepted within ★ 14 days of receipt, provided the item is unused and in its original packaging. In such cases:

  • If the return is due to our error (wrong or defective item): we cover return shipping costs.
  • If the return is due to a change of mind: the customer is responsible for return shipping costs.

8. Non-Refundable Items

The following are not eligible for refunds under any circumstances:

  • Delivery fees and service charges.
  • Custom orders where production has already begun.
  • Gift cards and store credits.
  • Orders placed through third-party platforms (Uber Eats).
  • Products showing signs of mishandling, improper storage, or consumption after delivery.

9. Chargebacks & Payment Disputes

We strongly encourage you to contact us directly before initiating a chargeback with your bank or card issuer. Most issues can be resolved quickly at info@cookiesbyjackie.com.

Our dispute process:

  • We will respond to all customer dispute inquiries within ★ 7 business days.
  • If a chargeback is filed with your card network (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, etc.) without prior contact, we reserve the right to contest the dispute and submit all available evidence, including order confirmation, delivery records, and communication logs, in accordance with applicable card network rules.
  • Under standard card network rules, disputes may be raised within 120 days of the transaction date (timeframes vary by network).
  • Chargebacks filed for orders that were delivered as described and meet our quality standards, without prior complaint, may be treated as fraud and reported accordingly.

To protect both parties, we recommend keeping all order confirmations and communication records.
