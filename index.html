COOKIES BY JACKIE

Desde su apertura en 2024, Cookies by Jackie se ha convertido rápidamente en un destino imprescindible para los amantes de los postres en la ciudad de Nueva York. En una ciudad que nunca se detiene, ofrece el momento perfecto de pausa: galletas recién horneadas, postres artesanales y bebidas diseñadas para reconectarte contigo mismo o con alguien especial.

Más que una pastelería, es una experiencia creada para la vida de NYC: acelerada, audaz y llena de momentos que vale la pena celebrar. Cada receta combina la tradición casera con ingredientes de alta calidad, ofreciendo sabores auténticos que destacan en el corazón de la ciudad.

Ya sea un antojo de media tarde, un regalo detallista o una celebración especial, Cookies by Jackie está hecha para aportar algo dulce y memorable a cada historia de Nueva York.

Desde 2024, Cookies by Jackie es el destino dulce imprescindible en Nueva York. Ofrecemos una experiencia única que combina tradición casera con la más alta calidad, perfecta para regalar o darte un momento de pausa en medio del ritmo de la ciudad.

Privacy Policy / Política de Privacidad

Last updated: June 2025

1. Who We Are

Cookies by Jackie ("we," "us," or "our") is an artisan bakery operating from Columbus Circle Underground Market, New York, NY 10019. You can reach us at info@cookiesbyjackie.com or +1 212-389-6151.

2. Information We Collect

  • Identity & contact data: name, email address, phone number, billing and shipping address.
  • Payment data: credit/debit card details collected and processed securely by Stripe, Inc. We never store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Transaction data: purchase history and order details.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, and cookies.
  • Usage data: how you interact with our website (pages visited, clicks).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill your orders.
  • To prevent fraud and verify payment authenticity.
  • To communicate with you about your orders and customer service.
  • To send promotional communications (only with your explicit consent).
  • To comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Payment Processing & Stripe

We use Stripe, Inc. as our payment processor. When you make a purchase, your payment card information is collected directly by Stripe through Stripe.js, a JavaScript library that runs in your browser and transmits your card data directly and securely to Stripe's servers — this sensitive data never passes through or is stored on our servers.

Powered by Stripe — PCI DSS Level 1 Certified

Stripe may collect and use your financial and personal data — including device fingerprint, IP address, and transaction history — to:

  • Process your payment securely and in accordance with applicable payment network rules.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent transactions on behalf of merchants and financial institutions.
  • Comply with financial regulations and anti-money-laundering (AML) requirements.

Stripe's use of your personal data is governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy. By completing a purchase, you consent to the transfer of your payment information to Stripe for the purposes described above, including the transfer of sensitive personal data to a third-party payment processor for fraud prevention purposes as required under applicable payment network rules.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Uber Eats: orders placed through UberEats are governed by UberEats' Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts: used to render website fonts; Google may collect technical data (IP address) per its Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare (FontAwesome CDN): used to serve icons; Cloudflare may collect technical data per its Privacy Policy.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS (TLS) connections. Our payment processor, Stripe, holds PCI DSS Level 1 certification — the highest standard in payment security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with our legal obligations (typically 7 years for financial and tax records), and resolve any disputes.

8. Your Rights Under GDPR (EU / EEA Residents)

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of access: request a copy of your personal data.
  • Right to rectification: request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"): request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to restriction of processing: request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability: receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object: object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.
  • Right to withdraw consent: withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

The legal bases for our data processing are: contract performance (order fulfillment), legitimate interests (fraud prevention and security), legal obligation (financial and tax regulations), and consent (marketing communications). To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@cookiesbyjackie.com.

9. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to know: request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected, used, or shared.
  • Right to delete: request deletion of personal information we have collected about you.
  • Right to opt-out of sale: we do not sell your personal information to third parties.
  • Right to non-discrimination: we will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.

To submit a CCPA request, contact us at info@cookiesbyjackie.com. We will respond within 45 calendar days.

10. Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies required for the website to function (e.g., shopping cart session state). We do not use advertising or behavioral tracking cookies. Stripe may use cookies and similar technologies on payment pages to prevent fraud and improve payment security, consistent with its own cookie policy.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a minor, please contact us immediately.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this document reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of our website following any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Con tecnología Stripe — Certificado PCI DSS Nivel 1

  • Cumplir con las regulaciones financieras y los requisitos contra el lavado de dinero (AML).

El uso de sus datos personales por parte de Stripe se rige por la Política de Privacidad de Stripe. Al completar una compra, usted consiente la transferencia de su información de pago a Stripe para los fines descritos, incluyendo la transferencia de datos personales sensibles a un procesador de pagos tercero para la prevención del fraude, conforme a las normas aplicables de las redes de pago.

Si se encuentra en la Unión Europea o el Espacio Económico Europeo, tiene los siguientes derechos bajo el Reglamento General de Protección de Datos (RGPD):

  • Derecho de acceso: solicitar una copia de sus datos personales.
  • Derecho de rectificación: solicitar la corrección de datos inexactos o incompletos.
  • Derecho de supresión ("derecho al olvido"): solicitar la eliminación de sus datos personales.
  • Derecho a la limitación del tratamiento: solicitar que limitemos el uso de sus datos.
  • Derecho a la portabilidad de los datos: recibir sus datos en un formato estructurado y legible por máquina.

  • Derecho a retirar el consentimiento: revocar el consentimiento en cualquier momento cuando el tratamiento se base en él, sin afectar a la licitud del tratamiento previo.

Las bases legales para nuestro tratamiento de datos son: ejecución de un contrato (cumplimiento de pedidos), intereses legítimos (prevención de fraude y seguridad), obligación legal (regulaciones financieras y fiscales), y consentimiento (comunicaciones de marketing). Para ejercer cualquiera de estos derechos, contáctenos en info@cookiesbyjackie.com.
